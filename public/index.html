      FAQ

      What do I get with the demo?

      A custom homepage design for your business, sent as a private preview link within 3 business days. It shows the look and feel of your site so you can decide before buying.

      Do I have to buy the full website?

      No. The demo has no obligation. If you don't want the full site, you don't pay anything more.

      Is the demo price credited?

      Yes. Your demo email includes a code that takes $9 off the full website at checkout.

      What's included in the full website?

      A custom design for your brand with up to 5 pages, built to work on phones and computers, 2 rounds of revisions, basic SEO setup (page titles, descriptions and a sitemap), and launch on a free web address or a domain you own.

      How long does the full website take?

      We deliver within 3 business days after we have the content we need from you, like your text, logo and photos. We can help write your text if you don't have it.

      Are hosting and a domain included?

      We launch your site on a free web address. If you want your own domain (like yourbusiness.com), you buy it from any domain registrar and we connect it for you. Paid third-party services, if you choose any, are your cost.

      Do I own my website?

      Yes. Once the full website is paid for, the finished site and the content you gave us are yours.

      Can you build an online store?

      The full website covers up to 5 pages. If you need a store that takes payments, email us before ordering and we'll confirm what's included.

      What if I'm not happy?

      Tell us what to change. The full website includes 2 rounds of revisions. See our refund policy for when refunds apply.
